# ResuAI

**Create Smarter Resumes, Portfolios & Career Paths — Powered by AI**

Welcome to **ResuAI**, a modern, intelligent, and free platform designed to help users build professional resumes and portfolios with the power of Artificial Intelligence. From resume generation to career coaching, ResuAI is your personal assistant in career development.

---

## 🚀 Features

### ✨ AI Resume Generator

Create tailored, ATS-friendly resumes effortlessly. Just answer a few questions or upload a basic draft — our AI will take care of the rest.

### 💬 AI Career Coach

Chat with your AI coach to receive:

* Resume feedback
* Interview preparation
* Career guidance
* Skill-building tips

### 🌐 AI Portfolio Builder

Build elegant, customizable, and responsive portfolio websites to showcase your:

* Projects
* Certifications
* Achievements
* Work experience

### 🌎 Multilingual Support

Communicate with the AI in multiple languages to make it globally accessible.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* **Framework**: Next.js (React-based full-stack application)
* **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* **Backend**: Node.js / Express (AI integration layer)
* **Authentication**: Firebase Authentication (Google Login)
* **Database**: Firestore (Real-time storage of user resumes and portfolio data)
* **AI Integration**: Gemini API (for resume, portfolio & coach logic)
* **Hosting**: Vercel / GitHub Pages

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Start for Free**: No login required. Just visit the site.
2. **Choose a Path**:

   * Resume Generator
   * AI Coach
   * Portfolio Builder
3. **Answer Questions or Chat**: Let the AI collect your data.
4. **Get Instant Output**: Download your resume or publish your portfolio.
